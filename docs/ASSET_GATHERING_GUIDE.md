## **📋 Asset Specifications:**

### **Image Assets:**
- **Resolution**: 512x512 minimum (for quality)
- **Format**: PNG with transparency
- **Color depth**: 32-bit (RGBA)
- **Style**: Consistent with your game's aesthetic

### **Audio Assets:**
- **Format**: WAV or OGG
- **Sample rate**: 44.1kHz
- **Bit depth**: 16-bit minimum
- **Length**: 1-3 seconds for effects

### **Animation Assets:**
- **Frame rate**: 30fps or 60fps
- **Format**: PNG sequence or GIF
- **Loop**: Seamless looping preferred
- **Style**: Smooth and professional
